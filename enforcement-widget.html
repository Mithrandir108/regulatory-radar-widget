FCA fines former finance directors of Carillion plc (in liquidation)

The FCA fined two former Carillion finance directors for their role in misleading company statements. Richard Adam was fined £232,800 and Zafar Khan £138,900 for being aware of serious financial troubles in Carillion's UK construction business but failing to reflect this in company announcements or alert the Board and audit committee.

FCA Today

FCA obtains £265,523.96 confiscation order against Collateral fraudster Andrew Currie

The FCA secured a confiscation order of £265,523.96 against Andrew Currie at Southwark Crown Court. Currie was previously convicted in 2023 and sentenced to 2 years 6 months imprisonment for defrauding investors through the collapsed peer-to-peer lending platform Collateral (UK) Ltd, diverting funds for personal gain including purchasing property in Spain.

FCA fines oil rig consultant £309,843 for insider dealing

The FCA fined Russel Gerrity £309,843 for using inside information to net himself £128,765. As a consultant with access to oil and gas drilling information, Gerrity used inside information between October 2018 and January 2022 to buy shares in Chariot Oil & Gas Limited and Eco (Atlantic) Oil and Gas Plc ahead of price-increasing announcements.

FCA Today

Tribunal upholds ban and fines for corrupt and dishonest adviser

The Upper Tribunal upheld the FCA's decision to ban Darren Antony Reynolds from working in financial services and fine him £2,037,892. Reynolds was found to be dishonest when giving pension transfer advice and investment recommendations, causing significant harm to his customers and showing clear disregard for their interests.
