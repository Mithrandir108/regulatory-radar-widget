FCA stops Verus Financial Services Limited from carrying out regulated activities and tightens asset restrictions

The FCA removed all regulatory permissions from Verus Financial Services Limited and imposed stricter asset restrictions. The enforcement action followed concerns that the firm repeatedly breached existing asset restrictions and failed to comply with a Financial Ombudsman Service decision.

FCA Today

FCA opens investigation into claims management company

The FCA opened an enforcement investigation into The Claims Protection Agency Limited (TCPA) following concerns about its advertising and sales tactics related to motor finance claims. The investigation focuses on what customers were told about potential redress amounts, whether they were informed they could make claims for free, and whether they were pressured to sign up.

FCA Today

FCA fines former finance directors of Carillion plc (in liquidation)

The FCA fined Richard Adam £232,800 and Zafar Khan £138,900 for their role in misleading statements issued by Carillion plc. Both former finance directors were aware of serious financial troubles in Carillion's UK construction business but failed to reflect this in company announcements or alert the Board and audit committee.

FCA Today

FCA obtains £265,523.96 confiscation order against Collateral fraudster Andrew Currie

The FCA secured a confiscation order of £265,523.96 against Andrew Currie, who was previously convicted and sentenced to 2 years 6 months imprisonment for defrauding investors through collapsed peer-to-peer lending platform Collateral (UK) Ltd. Currie diverted investor funds for personal use, including purchasing property in Spain.

The AMF Enforcement Committee fines an asset management company and its directors for breaches of their professional obligations

The AMF Enforcement Committee imposed fines on an asset management company and its directors for breaches of their professional obligations. The enforcement action targets violations of regulatory duties by both the company and its management personnel.
