FCA stops Verus Financial Services Limited from carrying out regulated activities and tightens asset restrictions

The FCA removed all regulatory permissions from Verus Financial Services Limited and imposed stringent asset restrictions. The enforcement action followed concerns that the firm repeatedly breached existing asset restrictions and failed to comply with a Financial Ombudsman Service decision.

FCA Today

FCA fines former finance directors of Carillion plc (in liquidation)

The FCA fined two former Carillion finance directors for misleading statements: Richard Adam £232,800 and Zafar Khan £138,900. Both were aware of serious financial troubles in Carillion's UK construction business but failed to reflect this in company announcements or alert the Board and audit committee.

FCA Today

FCA obtains £265,523.96 confiscation order against Collateral fraudster Andrew Currie

The FCA secured a confiscation order of £265,523.96 against Andrew Currie, who was convicted in 2023 and sentenced to 2 years 6 months imprisonment for defrauding investors through collapsed peer-to-peer lending platform Collateral (UK) Ltd. Currie diverted investor funds for personal use, including purchasing property in Spain.

FCA Today

FCA fines oil rig consultant £309,843 for insider dealing

The FCA fined Russel Gerrity £309,843 for insider dealing after he used confidential information about oil and gas discoveries to profit £128,765. Between October 2018 and January 2022, the consultant bought shares in Chariot Oil & Gas Limited and Eco (Atlantic) Oil and Gas ahead of price-moving announcements.

The AMF Enforcement Committee fines an asset management company and its directors for breaches of their professional obligations

The AMF Enforcement Committee imposed fines on an asset management company and its directors for breaches of their professional obligations. The sanctions targeted violations of regulatory requirements by the investment management firm and its leadership.
