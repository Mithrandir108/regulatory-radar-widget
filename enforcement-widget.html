New member appointed to the Regulatory Decisions Committee

The FCA Board has appointed Dan Lavender as a new member of its Regulatory Decisions Committee (RDC). The RDC is responsible for taking certain regulatory decisions on behalf of the FCA relating to contested enforcement action.
